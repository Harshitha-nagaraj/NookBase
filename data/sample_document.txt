RAG Debugger is a tool for developers to inspect Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines.
It provides insights into retrieved contexts, vector similarities, and potential grounding failures.
A typical RAG pipeline consists of document ingestion, text extraction, chunking, embedding generation, vector storage, and similarity retrieval.
SentenceTransformers is often used for generating lightweight dense embeddings.
ChromaDB is a popular open-source vector database used for local storage.
